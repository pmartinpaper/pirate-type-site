About

Being a pirate can be difficult in this day and age. With pesky rival pirates wanting to steal all your precious treasures, it can create a lot of stress on the body. This creates what is essentially an open drawbridge situation for diseases and sicknesses.

One of the most common quarrels fellow mateys face on the open seas, especially baby pirates, is seasickness. This infliction causes a lack of group efficiency and most importantly a lack of treasure. A great way to resolve this is to just put two handy pirate eyepatches over both of your eyes, so that way instead of feeling the full extent of the full waves, you just feel like you’re on a really badly built abode on land.

Now let's get into things that can not be covered by those trendy eyepatches. Dysentery and Scurvy. These are diseases that are pretty bad. Like really bad. The best way to cure these things is through prevention. You can prevent these ravenous diseases by making sure you wash your hands and practice overall a healthy amount of hygiene. You could be hesitating to do these things due to peer pressure from your fellow shipmates, but just remember if your hands are dirty, that means the treasure you handle will also be dirty, and no one wants dirty and germ-filled treasure.

Unfortunately one of the biggest diseases out there is The Plague. It's destructive, horrible, and causes misery all over the yee seas. The best way to deal with The Plague is to quarantine yourself from others while doctors take care of you on land. As a pirate you may be devastated to be away from your pirate family, but it is the best solution to protect yourself and your shipmates.
